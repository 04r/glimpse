Six days in the fjords

Photos and notes by Ana Ruiz, 8 min read

We took the slow ferry north and stopped wherever the water looked calm enough to swim. Day three was the one everyone asks about: a paper plane, a tailwind, and the longest glide any of us had seen.

DatesJune 12 to 18
Booking codeKJ-4471-PX
RouteBergen to Ålesund
FerryHurtigruten, deck 6

The days

Day 1Bergen fish market at seven, then the funicular before the crowds.
Day 2Ferry to Balestrand. Cold swim, warm cinnamon buns.
Day 3The glide. Someone filmed it. Nobody believes it.
Day 4Rain all morning, rainbow all afternoon.
