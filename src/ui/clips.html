Instant replay

Glimpse records in the background and keeps only the last few minutes. Press the save key after something good happens and those moments become a clip. Nothing is kept until you save.

Off
Turn it on to start keeping the last moments.

How much to keep

Replay length
Saving a clip takes this many seconds back from the moment you press the key
Short clip length
For the second save key, when you only want the last few seconds
Disk space while running
Kept in a temporary folder and wiped when it turns off

Keys

Keys work inside games too. If a key does nothing, another app (for example the NVIDIA or Xbox overlay) may already use it: pick different keys here or turn that overlay off.

When to run

Games and apps
Instant replay turns on while any of these is running, and off 30 seconds after they close

What to record

Screen

App
Only this window is recorded, wherever it is. Fullscreen games fill the whole screen. If the app isn't open, the screen is recorded until it is.

Quality

Quality
Bitrate
Megabits per second. 20 to 50 is plenty for 1080p at 60 fps
Resolution
Lower than your screen makes smaller files, the picture is scaled down
Frame rate
60 is smooth for most games. Higher only helps on high refresh screens

Encoder Advanced

Format
H.264 plays everywhere, including Discord. HEVC is the same quality at about 30% smaller, but some apps can't play it
Encoder
Graphics card encoders cost your game almost nothing. The CPU one slows games down
Bitrate mode
Quality level
Lower is better and bigger. 18 looks near perfect, 28 is noticeably soft
Encoder speed
Slower presets look a little better and use more of the graphics card
